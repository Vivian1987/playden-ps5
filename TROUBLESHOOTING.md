### TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
“Play not prepared” and/or “The compatibility kit is missing or incomplete”.

Solution: (figured out from logs)
Grab the manifest.json from the kit folder in your ftp server/your computer if you haven't sent the folder to the ps5 yet, and find the firmware field in the file

Change the firmware field from 13.60 to your firmware version (ex: 9.40, 7.61)

Then send the manifest.json to your ps5.
